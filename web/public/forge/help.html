Stuck transaction?

Most go through in seconds. If one doesn't, work down this list.

1. Check it

  • In your wallet's activity, open the transaction and view it on the explorer.
  • Pending: it hasn't landed yet. Success: it's done, reload the Forge. Failed: nothing changed but the network fee.
  • Don't send it again while one is pending. Both could go through.

2. Speed it up or cancel

  • In your wallet, open the pending transaction and pick Speed up or Cancel. Both cost a small network fee.
  • Newer transactions wait behind a stuck one. Clear that one first.

3. Wrong network

  • The Forge only works on Robinhood Chain. If your wallet is on another network, switch it and try again.

4. Approvals

  • Paying in PLANK or USDG takes two steps: approve, then buy. If only step 1 went through, do step 2.
  • The Forge only asks to approve the exact amount, never more.

5. Opening and grading

  • Opening burns the pack, then drand randomness deals your cards, usually within a few minutes. Grading works the same way. There's no re-roll.
  • If randomness stops for 7 days, anyone can cancel: packs come back sealed, cards come back ungraded. A grading fee isn't refunded: it was already burned.

6. Reloading is safe

  • The Forge remembers a pending transaction and picks it back up after a reload.

Never share your seed phrase or private key. Nobody needs it to help you. A transaction hash (it starts with 0x) is all anyone needs.
