Terms & risks

Plain and short. Using the Forge means you accept these.

Right now the Forge is a demo. Nothing is real: no wallet, no payments.

What you get

  • Packs and cards are NFTs on Robinhood Chain. They're collectibles, for fun.
  • They're not an investment. Nothing here promises value, profit or a resale.
  • Each Series' odds are fixed before it sells. A pack can still be all commons.

Risks

  • Prices move. PLANK, PAPER, ETH and cards can all drop to zero.
  • Transactions are final. Nobody can reverse, refund or recover them, us included.
  • Contracts can have bugs. Ours are tested and audited internally, not by an outside firm.
  • The owner can pause buying (Press and free packs too) and case and grade payments. Opening, dealing, transfers and finishing grades never pause.
  • The owner can switch the randomness source, announced first. Only new opens and grades use it.
  • Wallets, marketplaces, the chain and drand are run by others. If they stop, parts of the Forge stop too.
  • Raw cards wear with time and moves. Grades are final.

Your wallet

  • We never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is a scammer.
  • Read every request in your wallet before you sign it.

Fees

  • A pack is its price plus PAPER, which is burned. Each Series sets both. Network fees are extra.
  • Case and grade fees buy PAPER and burn it.
  • Resales pay a royalty (never over 10%), on marketplaces that honour it.

The rest

  • Follow your local laws and pay any taxes that apply. Don't use the Forge where it isn't allowed.
  • The Forge is provided as is, with no warranty.
