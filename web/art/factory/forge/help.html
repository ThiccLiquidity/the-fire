Stuck transaction?

Most land in seconds. If not, try these.

1. Check it

  • Find it in your wallet's activity, then view it on the explorer.
  • Pending: wait. Success: reload. Failed: only the network fee was spent.
  • Don't send it again while one is pending. Both could go through.

2. Speed it up or cancel

  • In your wallet, open the pending transaction and pick Speed up or Cancel. Both cost a small network fee.
  • Newer transactions wait behind a stuck one. Clear that one first.

3. Wrong network

  • Switch your wallet to Robinhood Chain and try again.

4. Approvals

  • Paying with a coin other than ETH takes 2 taps: approve, then buy. A pack takes PAPER too, so up to 3.
  • An approval landed but the purchase didn't? Try again. It picks up from there.
  • Each approval is capped at what that one purchase can take (the quote + 3%). Never unlimited.
  • Your wallet may show up to 3% more than the price. Anything unused comes back.

5. Opening and grading

  • Cards and grades arrive in about 90 seconds. Grades are final.
  • Stuck for 24 hours? Anyone can cancel: packs return sealed, cards ungraded. Grading fees aren't refunded: they're already burned.

6. Reloading is safe

  • Cardworks remembers a pending transaction and picks it back up after a reload.

Never share your seed phrase or private key. To get help, share the transaction link.
