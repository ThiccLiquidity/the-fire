# Card contracts: internal security review (October 2026)

Two internal review rounds, each split into independent areas, plus fuzz and invariant tests. No external audit has
been done; the plan before launch is a full testnet run and a small first drop.

- **Scope:** `contracts/src/cards/` (FirePacks, FireCards, CardRules, FireSale, FirePsa), their use of the drand
  adapter and router, and `script/DeployCards.s.sol`.
- **Round 1 areas:**
  - Sale money flows
  - Sale rules and state
  - Cards and packs
  - PDA reveal and randomness
  - Deployment and trust
- **Method:** every finding needed a Foundry test that showed it. Each fix has a regression test named `test_audit_*`.

**Result:** no way to steal funds or get funds stuck. 14 findings were fixed, and a few risks are accepted (below).

## Round 1: accepted (known and documented)

- **Holding the PLANK pool for 30 minutes.** Someone who keeps the 28-WETH PLANK pool off its price for a full
  30 minutes, then checkpoints, can buy packs a little cheaper. This costs far more in arbitrage losses than the gain
  (at most about $1.25 a pack).
- **Sandwiching the burn swap.** The swap floor (90% of the average) lets about 10% of a burn share be taken by
  sandwiching. That's minor on an L2 with a first-come sequencer.
- **The last pack of a Fire is predictable.** It gets exactly what's left in the public pool. This comes with
  exact totals, and is documented.
- **The keeper is load-bearing.** It must checkpoint the PLANK price every 30 minutes and deliver drand numbers
  right away. Without it:
  - PLANK purchases pause, and the burn share goes to the burn wallet
  - opens and reveals wait
  - a holder who can see their drand number could re-roll after a full day
- **Owner trust that remains:**
  - the owner sets each drop before it opens
  - the owner can end a drop after 48h
  - the owner chooses which suggestions are picked
  - the owner can change pack art and royalty (≤ 10%)
  - the revenue and burn wallets are the owner's: "the burn wallet only burns" is a promise, checkable on chain
- **A wallet that refuses ETH stops ETH sales.** A Safe accepts ETH.
- **USDG route.** The burn swap goes USDG → WETH → PLANK. If there is no USDG/WETH V2 pool, the USDG burn share always
  goes to the burn wallet. Check this on chain before launch.

## Round 2: fresh review, plus fuzz and invariant tests

Reviewers who hadn't seen round 1 covered four areas:
- the sale, from an attacker's side
- cards, PDA and randomness
- code compared with this doc, line by line
- trust and operations

**Fuzz and invariant tests** (`contracts/test/invariant/`): random sequences of buys, starters, credits, burns,
opens, reveals, time jumps and router failures, run by several actors, checking:
- the sale never holds funds
- every PAPER spent is burned
- revenue, burn wallet and PLANK burned add up exactly per currency
- credits are exactly floor(burned / 42) plus picks
- supply is never exceeded, and wallet limits hold
- every pack has 3 Paper, a Wood and a Fire-or-better
- a fully dealt Fire matches its pool exactly
- grades are 1–10 and set once
- a card waiting on its grade never moves

All pass. They found no contract bugs.

**Round 2: fixed** (regression tests `test_audit2_*`):

| Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| High | **Unsold packs stranded.** If a drop never sold out and the owner never ended it, its packs could never be opened. | Anyone can end it 7 days after the wallet limit lifts. |
| Medium | **No max PAPER on mints.** Buyers had no maximum on the PAPER part, so `paperPerPack` could be raised just before the start. | Every mint takes `maxPaper`. |
| Medium | **Credits skipped the rules.** Credits ignored the PLANK-only phase and the wallet limit. | Credits wait for PLANK to light the forge and count toward the limit. |

| Medium | **Lost if drand stops for good.** Opened packs and cards waiting on a grade were lost forever. | After 7 days with no answer, `cancelOpen` returns the packs sealed and `cancelReveal` unlocks the cards. |
| Medium | **Characters changeable mid-drop.** A Fire's characters (and so the cap on picks) could change during its drop. | Fixed once its first pack is minted. |
| Low | **Close order steered rare cards.** Two drops at once let the close order steer a carried rare card. | One drop at a time. |
| Low | **Picked credits could expire.** A picked credit was lost if its drop sold out first. | It converts to an ordinary credit (`convertPickCredits`). |
| Low | **PDA price fell back too easily.** The PDA price fell back to the starting number when nobody had revealed while PAPER had a price, and the feed couldn't be added if deployed without one. | Keeper `pokePrice()`; `setPaperFeed` once. |
| Low | **Weak deploy check.** The deploy didn't check the router's WETH and factory against the PLANK pool. | Checked. |

**Round 2: accepted (documented):**
- **Deployer window.** Until the multisig accepts ownership, the deployer key controls packs, cards and PDA. The
  multisig accepts right after the deploy and checks the wiring (see `docs/cards-contracts.md`, Deploying).
- **Wallets that refuse ETH.** A revenue or burn wallet that refuses ETH stops ETH sales. Use a Safe.
- **PAPER blocking `0xdead`.** If PAPER ever paused or blocked `0xdead`, minting would pause until that's fixed.
  Drops can still be ended so packs open.
- **Owner keys lost.** Ongoing drops can still be ended by anyone after the grace period.
- **Keeper duties:**
  - checkpoint the PLANK price every 30 minutes
  - deliver drand numbers
  - call `FirePsa.pokePrice()` now and then

## Changes after round 2

- **Bots on the paid sale.** Round 2 noted that one contract could create throwaway wallets and buy a whole drop in
  one transaction. Two rules were added:
  - For the first 24 hours (set per drop), only wallets with a Paper Press (each press once per drop) or in the
    $69-PLANK snapshot (Merkle root from `ops/snapshot`) can buy paid packs.
  - While the wallet limit is on, only regular wallets can buy (`msg.sender == tx.origin`), so one bot contract
    can't sweep a drop. Tests: `test_holderWindow_*`, `test_noBotContractsWhileTheLimitIsOn`,
    `test_snapshotScriptRootMatches`.
- **What "packs" means.** `configureDrop`'s `packs` is paid packs only; starters come on top. A 167-pack drop with
  50 starters is configured as 117 + 50.
- **Free pack credits work at any time in any live drop.** This reverses round 2's credit rule. A big credit holder
  can mint at a drop's start, ahead of the PLANK-only packs. Picked credits are ordinary credits, which is safe
  because there's one drop at a time and picks only happen before a drop opens.
- **Each Series stands alone.** The carried rarity accumulators are gone: every Series makes at least 1 Diamond
  (more if set with `setDiamonds`), Fire and Coal take their share of that Series, Wood the rest.
