# Wild years

Random, moody buying over a year, through the exact contract mirror (`python3 sim/wild_year.py`; details in its header): moods from dead to hype lasting days to weeks, FOMO that grows with the pot, a "save it" rush for old fires with big pots, dry spells of 5-14 days, whales, noisy days, busier weekends, and a PLANK price that wanders 6%/day with pumps and dumps. $250 seed, 40/25/5/30, 5x prize cap, 3/2/1 free logs.

Two moods of the world: **grounded** (centered on 5-100 logs a day, FOMO capped at 4x) and **wild** (hype common, FOMO feeds on itself).
