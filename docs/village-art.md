# The village: art prompts

The landing page is a village. Five buildings and two empty lots around a plaza:

| Spot | Building | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Top center (biggest) | **Workshop** | the workshop scene (buy, open, burn, case and grade, suggest) |
| Left | **Arcade** | the Game room |
| Right | **Marketplace** | the market |
| Lower left | **Morning Wood** (print shop) | the daily paper |
| Lower right | **Trophy Hall** | showcase, binder, badges, pop report (stats later) |
| Far left, far right | two **empty lots** | buildings added later |
| Center | **the plaza** with the infinity monument | the burn tally sits on it |

## How to make it (so every piece matches)

1. **Master scene**: prompt 1 (desktop) and prompt 2 (phone). All five buildings in one picture, so the style,
   light and angle match. Make a few and pick the best.
2. **Empty background**: prompt 3, done as an edit of the chosen master (inpaint / "edit image"), not a new picture.
3. **Send me the master and the empty background.** I cut each building out of the master as its own layer (so it can
   glow and lift on hover), line it up on the background, and put the names and live news on top as site text.
4. **Seasons**: prompt 4, also edits of the master.
5. **New buildings later**: prompt 5, then I place it on an empty lot.

Rules for every prompt: **no words, letters or numbers anywhere** (signs are blank; the site writes the names), no
logos, no people's faces up close, nothing cut off at the edges.

## The style line (paste into every prompt)

> Cozy steampunk village at dusk, warm lamplight, dark weathered timber, aged brass and copper fittings, glowing amber
> windows, soft chimney smoke, mossy cobblestones. Polished 3D game-art render, isometric three-quarter view from
> above, soft cinematic lighting, rich warm color, crisp detail, clean readable shapes. Same world as a cozy steampunk
> card-printing workshop with a furnace, brass pipes and a printing press.

## 1. Master scene, desktop (16:9, 3840 x 2160 or the largest the tool gives)

> [style line] A small village hub seen from above at a three-quarter isometric angle. In the center, a round
> cobblestone plaza with a bronze figure-eight sculpture on a low stone plinth (an infinity shape, no text). Five
> buildings face the plaza on short cobbled paths:
> TOP CENTER, the largest: a two-storey timber-and-brick workshop with a big brass gear emblem on its gable, tall brick
> chimney with smoke, warm forge glow through the windows and the open double doors, stacks of paper rolls and timber by
> the door.
> LEFT: a small arcade hall, dark timber with a purple neon game-controller sign shape (just the shape, no letters),
> glowing purple and amber windows, a little bell tower.
> RIGHT: a market hall with a red-and-cream striped awning over open stalls, crates of cards and wrapped packs on the
> counters, hanging brass scales, lanterns.
> LOWER LEFT: a narrow newspaper print shop, a rolled-paper press visible through a big front window, bundles of
> newspapers tied with string on the step, a small newsboy stand with a blank sign.
> LOWER RIGHT: a small stone-and-timber trophy hall with columns, glass display windows lit from inside showing framed
> cards on stands, a brass dome roof.
> FAR LEFT and FAR RIGHT edges: two empty fenced grass lots with a small blank wooden sign post on each.
> A cobbled path leads out of the bottom of the picture (the way in), and another path leads off the right edge into
> trees. Trees, shrubs, lamp posts, a small water wheel on a stream at the left. Each building has a blank wooden
> signboard over its door. Leave clear sky and empty ground around every building so each one can be cut out
> cleanly; buildings must not overlap each other. Keep everything inside the frame with a margin.
> No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere.

## 2. Master scene, phone (9:16, 1440 x 2560 or larger)

> [style line] The same village hub, composed for a tall phone screen, seen from above at a three-quarter isometric
> angle. Stacked from top to bottom: the large workshop (brass gear gable, smoking chimney, forge glow) at the top
> center; below it, the round cobblestone plaza with the bronze figure-eight sculpture; left of the plaza the arcade
> hall (purple neon game-controller shape, no letters), right of the plaza the market hall (red-and-cream striped
> awning, stalls of cards and packs); below them, lower left the newspaper print shop (press in the window, bundled
> papers on the step), lower right the trophy hall (columns, lit display windows of framed cards, brass dome). Two
> small empty fenced lots at the very bottom corners. A cobbled path leads in from the bottom edge. Blank wooden
> signboards over every door. Buildings never overlap; clear space around each one; everything inside the frame with
> a margin. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere.

## 3. Empty background (edit of the chosen master, same size)

> Remove the five buildings and replace each with what was under it: a flat cobblestone or packed-earth foundation pad
> with a little grass at the edges, in the same light and perspective. Keep everything else exactly the same: the
> plaza and its sculpture, paths, trees, lamp posts, stream, the two empty lots and the sky. No text.

## 4. Seasons (edits of the chosen master and its empty background, same size and layout)

**Winter:**
> Same scene, same layout and angle, at night in winter: thick snow on the roofs, paths swept clear, snow-dusted trees,
> icicles, warm windows glowing brighter against blue moonlight, light snowfall, wreaths with red ribbon on the doors,
> string lights between the lamp posts. No text.

**Festival (a Series launch):**
> Same scene, same layout and angle, at sunset during a festival: triangle bunting strung across the plaza, small
> striped tents and lanterns along the paths, a red carpet up the main path to the workshop, confetti, a steampunk
> airship with a brass gondola floating in the sky above the workshop. No text.

## 5. A new building later (to place on an empty lot)

> [style line] A single [describe it: e.g. "a stone-and-timber harbor master's office with a lighthouse lamp on the
> roof"] on a small square cobblestone pad, seen from above at a three-quarter isometric angle, the same angle and the
> same warm dusk light coming from the upper left as the rest of the village. Blank wooden signboard over the door.
> The building fills about 70% of the picture, centered, on a plain flat light-grey background (or transparent) so it
> can be cut out. No text, letters, numbers or logos. Square, 2048 x 2048.

Send it with the master scene so I can match its size and light when I place it.
