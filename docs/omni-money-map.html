Rules the contracts always keep

Never hold funds

Everything paid is forwarded or burned in the same transaction. There's no pot, no withdraw button and nothing to steal.

Supply locks at the first sale

You set every number per drop: packs, price, limits, timers, odds. Once a pack sells, none can change.

Contents are secret until opened

A pack's cards don't exist until it's opened and drand's random number arrives. Nobody can peek, including you.

Buyers name their max

Every purchase carries the most the buyer agreed to pay. If a price moved, the purchase fails and costs nothing.

A mint never fails on PLANK

If the PLANK swap can't go through, the 30% goes to the burn wallet to burn later and the purchase still succeeds.

Dealt cards can't be changed

Once minted, a card is final. Only the PSA reveal changes it, by setting its grade once.
