## 1. Introducing Omni Cards (main post)

> **Omni Cards are here.** Trading cards on Robinhood Chain, made in the Omni Cardworks.
>
> Every Series is a small, fixed run: about 1,000 cards in sealed packs. When a Series sells out, that's it. No
> reprints, ever.
>
> **What's in a pack.** Cards come in Paper, Wood, Fire and Coal, with rare Gold and one-of-a-kind Full Art cards.
> Any card can come out holo: on the frame, on the picture, or both. Packs open on chain with public drand
> randomness, so nobody (us included) knows what's inside until it's opened.
>
> **Grade your cards.** Keep them raw, put them in a case, or send them for a PDA grade (1 to 10). Raw cards wear
> over time; a case stops the clock; a grade seals the card in a slab for good.
>
> **Burn what you don't want.** Every 42 cards burned earns a free pack.
>
> **You decide what's on the cards.** Every Series' theme, art style and characters are suggested by the community.
> The first suggestion box opens [DATE]. Get picked and you win a free pack.
>
> Packs are $2.50, paid in PLANK, ETH or USDG, plus 1 PAPER. Paper Press holders and PLANK holders get the first 24
> hours.
